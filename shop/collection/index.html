Dartboards

Looking for something that will get your friends and family into the competitive spirit on nights in? A dartboard doesn't take up much space and you can have hours of fun playing different games. Our range of high-quality boards covers smart Bluetooth boards, electronic soft-tip boards, traditional bristle boards and kids boards — all made in DAYIN's own factory in Dongguan.

How do I choose the right dartboard?

The two main types of dartboard are bristle (steel-tip) and electronic (soft-tip). Bristle boards are the traditional choice: sisal fibres self-heal after every throw and you play with steel-tip darts. Electronic boards use plastic soft-tip darts, score every dart automatically and — on our Smart series — connect to the DAYIN Sport app for online play.

If you are buying for a family or a flat, soft-tip is quieter and kinder to walls. If you play in a league, a 15.5″ regulation electronic board or a 17.75″ bristle board keeps you match-ready.

How to tell if a dartboard is good quality

On a bristle board, look for compressed sisal, a staple-free bullseye and ultra-thin wire so darts sit in the board instead of bouncing out. On an electronic board, look at the segment matrix: how quiet it is, how many darts it registers without misses, and whether the spider (the web between segments) is thin. DAYIN builds the segment matrices behind many boards on the market — the same matrix is in every board we sell under our own name.
