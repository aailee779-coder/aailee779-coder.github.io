Questions

Before you buy.

Soft-tip or steel-tip?

Electronic and smart boards use soft-tip (plastic) darts — quieter, safer for walls, and scored automatically. Bristle boards use steel-tip darts and a manual or module scoreboard.

Do I need the app?

Only for Smart and Glow boards, and only if you want online play and saved stats. Every electronic board scores on its own screen.

Where does it ship from?

DAYIN’s warehouse in Dongguan, China. Free over $150. Demo store: no orders are processed.
