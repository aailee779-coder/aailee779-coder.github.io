Sport

DAYIN Sport is the direct-to-consumer store of DAYIN, the Dongguan manufacturer that has built electronic dartboards for 50+ brands in 30+ countries. We stock smart Bluetooth boards, 13.5″ and 15.5″ electronic boards, bristle boards, kids boards, cabinets and darts — same segment matrix, same factory, our name on the box.

Founded in 1989, the group runs 100+ injection molding machines across Dongguan and Bắc Ninh, Vietnam. Six product lines — Smart, 13.5″, 15.5″, commercial machines, traditional soft-tip and accessories — ship to 150+ clients worldwide. Venue and coin-op machines are sold through our sister brand DARTMAN Games.

Our personalized service lets clubs, bars and teams put their own logo on boards, cabinets and flights. Demo store: product names, prices and stock are sample data.
