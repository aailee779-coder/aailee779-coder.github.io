Shipping & returns

How long does delivery take?

Standard 5–9 working days, express 2–4. Orders dispatch from Dongguan within 1–2 working days. Demo store — no orders are processed.

What does shipping cost?

Free on orders over $150. Otherwise $12 standard, $29 express.

Can I return a board?

Yes — 30 days, in original packaging. Contact support for a label.

Warranty

What is covered?

Two years on electronic boards and cabinets, one year on bristle boards, against manufacturing defects. Segments are consumables and are sold as spares.

How do I claim?

Photos, order number, a short description — the form below. Because we make the board, we can usually send the exact module rather than a replacement unit.

App setup

Which boards use the app?

Smart Board S1 and Glow LED. Pair over Bluetooth 5.0; the app handles online matches, stats and firmware updates.

Can I play someone in another country?

Yes — online 1v1 works on any Smart board with an internet-connected phone.

Wholesale & OEM

Do you sell to shops and distributors?

Yes. DAYIN supplies retailers and brands worldwide. OEM/ODM enquiries go to DAYIN manufacturing; commercial venue machines to DARTMAN Games.
