The league flywheel

Where there’s a league,
the machine stays.

The machine is the entry ticket. The league is the reason it never leaves.

1

Players sign in

Every game is logged to a profile in the eDARTS app. Anonymous throws become known regulars.

2

Your venue is on the map

Players looking for a board find you. Your machine is a destination, not furniture.

3

Season runs

Weekly league night — same tables, same night, predictable bar revenue. Online matches against other venues.

4

Rankings feed back

Venue leaderboards and rankings pull players back in. Then you add the second machine.

Day one

Installed by one person.
Repaired by module.

EDmini arrives in three cartons, assembles without tools and stands on 58 × 35 cm of floor. When something fails, the module is swapped — the machine stays on the floor.
