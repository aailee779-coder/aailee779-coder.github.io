DARTMAN Games — the electronic dart machine

DARTMAN Games is the dart-machine brand of the DAYIN group — injection molding and electronics assembly in Dongguan since 1989, with a second plant in Bắc Ninh, Vietnam since 2024. EDmini is our flagship: the slimmest and lightest commercial dart machine, at 11 cm from segment to wall and 65 kg net.

Built for venues

A 21.5″ touchscreen, dual cameras, 4-speaker surround sound and backlight effects. A storage compartment, optional coin slot and modular repair keep it earning. Store management, ad rotation, game promotion, coin settings and happy-hour settings are built in.

We deliver worldwide

Each machine ships as three flat cartons — 85 machines per 20′ container, 180 per 40′. One person installs it without tools.

13 games, one board

01 (301–1501), Cricket, Count-Up, Bull Hunter, Random Checkout, Shooting Frisbee, Super Bull, Bingo, Soccer-PK, Balloon, Fireworks, Match and Online play. Players track scores, history and profiles in the eDARTS app; SDBplay 2 covers home boards.

Dealers & operators

We sell factory-direct and through a dealer network. Operators get a management back-end, spare-parts supply and a machine that stacks for storage and transport.

Personalize

Five finishes — Black, Gold, Silver, Pink and Blue — plus your logo on the idle screen and cabinet. Ask us about venue-branded runs.
