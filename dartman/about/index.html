About

A dart brand
built inside a factory.

Most dart-machine brands source their cabinets. DARTMAN is the brand of the people who mold them.

DARTMAN Games is the electronic-darts brand of the DAYIN group — a privately owned injection-molding manufacturer founded in Dongguan in 1989, with 100+ injection molding machines, 600+ staff and a 50+ person R&D team.

The EDmini was designed from the mold up: the head geometry, the 11 cm depth, the three-carton packaging and the modular repair concept are all decisions a factory makes, not a distributor.

In 2024 DAYIN opened a second plant in Bắc Ninh, Vietnam, with machines from 90 to 1200 tonnes — so EDmini can ship from either origin depending on the market.
